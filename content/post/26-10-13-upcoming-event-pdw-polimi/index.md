The School of Management of Politecnico di Milano will host a **Paper Development Workshop** with editors of *Organization Science*, *Strategic Management Journal* and *Research Policy* on 13 November 2026.

<!--more-->

The one-day workshop is sponsored by the Academy of Management Technology & Innovation Management (AOM TIM) Division. It offers PhD students and early-career researchers developmental feedback on their papers in progress from journal editors and senior scholars, to help move their manuscripts towards top-tier management and innovation journals. Topics include strategy, the economics and management of innovation, and organizations.

**Editors**

- **Giada Di Stefano** – Bocconi University, Associate Editor, *Strategic Management Journal*
- **Lamar Pierce** – Olin Business School, Washington University in St. Louis, Editor-in-Chief, *Organization Science*
- **John Walsh** – Politecnico di Milano, Editor, *Research Policy*

**Programme**

- Opening session in which the three editors discuss publishing in their journals
- Parallel sessions in which selected authors present their papers and receive detailed feedback from editors and faculty discussants
- Networking with editors, faculty and other participants

The workshop is free of charge.

**Key dates**

- Submission deadline: 24 September 2026
- Notification of acceptance: 13 October 2026
- Workshop: 13 November 2026, 09:00–17:00

The workshop is organized by Stefano Baruffaldi and Marianna Marra (Politecnico di Milano).

More information in the event [homepage](https://www.som.polimi.it/en/events/paper-development-workshop-organization-science-strategic-management-journal-research-policy/).
