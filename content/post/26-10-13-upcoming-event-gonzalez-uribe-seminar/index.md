On 1 December, the next MInERS seminar will take place at the University of Milan – La Statale.

<!--more-->

Our guest speaker will be prof. Juanita González-Uribe, Associate Professor of Finance at the London School of Economics and Political Science (LSE). Her research focuses on entrepreneurship, innovation, and private equity. The seminar will be followed by the usual aperitivo. This seminar series builds a platform for researchers specializing in the fields of economics of science, innovation and entrepreneurship in Milan to connect and collaborate.

The title of the talk and the registration details will be announced soon on the [event page](https://miners-research-seminars.eu/event/26-12-01-seminar-gonzalez-uribe-lse/).
